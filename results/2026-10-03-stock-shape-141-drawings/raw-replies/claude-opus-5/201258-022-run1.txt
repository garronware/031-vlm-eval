## Bounding Stock — Rectangular

**Part:** DET 022, INDICATOR BLOCK (P/N 201258, 1018 steel, black oxide, ground)

Because the part is an L‑shaped (stepped) block with flat gaging faces and side‑drilled/tapped holes, it would be cut from **rectangular bar stock**, not round.

| Bounding Dimension | Metric | Inch |
|---|---|---|
| **Thk** | 46.0 mm | **1.81 in** |
| **W** | 47.6 mm | **1.87 in** |
| **L** | 54.0 mm | **2.13 in** |

**Bounding cuboid: 1.81 × 1.87 × 2.13 in (46.0 × 47.6 × 54.0 mm)**

How these were derived:
- **46.0 [1.81]** – overall depth called out in the top/plan view.
- **47.6 [1.87/1.88]** – overall width; confirmed by the two 1/4 SHCS holes at 7.94 [.312] and 39.69 [1.562] from the 0 datum (symmetric about the 23.81 [.937] centerline).
- **54.0 [2.13]** – overall height in the side view (the 38.1 [1.50] and 20.6 [.81] dims define the step/notch, and the 3.2 [.12] × 45° chamfer and R1.5 corners fall inside this envelope).

For actual material purchase, add grind/clean‑up stock — e.g., a piece of 2" × 2-1/4" 1018 flat cut ~1.95" long (≈ 1.90 × 1.95 × 2.22 in rough) would cover the finished envelope.
