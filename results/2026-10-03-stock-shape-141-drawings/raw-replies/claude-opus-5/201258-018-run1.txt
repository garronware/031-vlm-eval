## Bounding Stock — Round Stock

This part is a circular "REST RING" (Det. #018), so it would be made from round bar/tube stock.

| Dimension | Metric | Imperial |
|---|---|---|
| **Diameter** | Ø69.9 mm | Ø2.750 in |
| **Length (thickness)** | 12.70 mm | 0.500 in |

**Basis:**
- **Diameter** — governed by the largest feature, the Ø69.9 [2.75] outside diameter of the flange (the Ø47.32 [1.863] is the raised boss/gaging face, and Ø33.337 [1.3125] is the through bore).
- **Length** — governed by the overall stack height of 12.70 [.500] shown in the side/section view.

*(Note: for actual ordering you'd typically bump to the next standard size, e.g. Ø2.875 in × 0.625 in long, to allow cleanup on the OD and both faces.)*
