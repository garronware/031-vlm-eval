## Bounding Stock — Round

This part is a solid disc/puck (turned cylindrical body with a 15° chamfer at the top O.D. and drilled/tapped holes in the face), so it would be made from **round stock**.

| Dimension | Metric | Inch |
|---|---|---|
| **Diameter** | 105.50 mm (max Ø) | 4.154 in |
| **Length (height)** | 50.8 mm | 2.00 in |

**Bounding cylinder: Ø105.5 mm × 50.8 mm long (Ø4.154 in × 2.00 in)**

Notes:
- The 15° × 6.4 mm [.25] chamfer is contained within the O.D./length envelope, so it does not increase the bounding size.
- Features (Ø5.000 dowel S.F. hole and three M5 × 0.8 holes) are internal and do not affect the envelope.
- Finished Ø is toleranced 105.50 ⁺⁰·⁰⁰/₋₀.₀₅; add typical machining allowance (e.g., ~3–6 mm on dia. and ~3 mm on length) when ordering bar stock — a Ø4.25 in (or Ø110 mm) bar cut ~2.25 in long is a practical purchase size.
