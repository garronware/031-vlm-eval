**Basis:** The part is a simple rectangular block with only tapped holes (4× M6×1 ↧12.0 and 4× M8×1.25 ↧16.0) — no turned features — so rectangular (plate/flat bar) stock applies. Finished envelope is 2.75 × 2.75 × 1.50 in.

**Sanity check:** 2.75 × 2.75 × 1.50 = 11.34 in³ × 0.284 lb/in³ ≈ 3.22 lb gross, which matches the 3.10 lb listed mass after deducting the eight tapped holes.

**Note for purchasing:** add machining allowance if all six faces are to be finished — e.g. order 1.625 × 3.00 × 3.00 in (or 1-3/4 × 3 × 3 bar) to clean up to size.
