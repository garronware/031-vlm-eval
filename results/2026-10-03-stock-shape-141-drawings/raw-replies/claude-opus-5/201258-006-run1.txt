**Basis:**
- The part is a turned/ground shaft — a single cylindrical body, so round stock applies.
- **Diameter:** The largest OD on the part is Ø6.350 [.2500]; the only other diameters (5.842 [.2300] ground journal, 0.74 [.029] feature in Detail A, M2.5 tapped hole) are smaller/internal.
- **Length:** Overall end-to-end dimension is 131.24 [5.167] from the "0" datum at the left end face; the 127.3 [5.01] and 25.4 [1.00] / 19.1 [.75] are intermediate feature locations.

*Note: raw bar would typically be ordered slightly oversize (e.g., 1/4″ or 7 mm dia × ~5.3″) to allow for cutoff, facing, and the grind/black-anodize operations called out.*
