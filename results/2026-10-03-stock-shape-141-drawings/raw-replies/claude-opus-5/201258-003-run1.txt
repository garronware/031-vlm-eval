## Bounding Stock — Rectangular (plate/bar stock)

This part ("FINGER," DET 003) is a prismatic machined block with a step, a chamfered nose, a through dowel hole and a large R45 gaging radius — it would be cut from rectangular bar, not round stock.

| Dimension | Metric | Inch |
|---|---|---|
| **Thickness** (part width, S.F. in Det. #008) | 12.70 mm | 0.500 in |
| **Width** (overall height in front view) | 15.7 mm | 0.62 in |
| **Length** | 44.4 mm | 1.75 in |

**Minimum stock size:** ≈ 12.70 × 15.7 × 44.4 mm (0.50 × 0.62 × 1.75 in), plus clean‑up allowance (e.g. 0.56 × 0.69 × 1.81 in) since all gaging faces are ground after carburize/harden to 58‑62 Rc.

**Sanity check:** 0.50 × 0.62 × 1.75 = 0.542 in³ of 1018 steel ≈ 0.153 lb; after removing the step, chamfers, radius and Ø0.250 hole this lands at the 0.12 lb listed on the drawing — consistent with these bounding dimensions.
