## Bounding Stock — Round

This part is a turned **spindle**, so it would be made from **round bar stock**.

| Dimension | Metric | Imperial |
|---|---|---|
| **Diameter** | Ø69.8 mm | Ø2.750 in |
| **Length** | 148.65 mm | 5.852 in |

**Basis:**
- **Diameter** — governed by the flange at the left end, called out as **ø69.8 [ø2.75]** (the largest feature on the part; all other diameters — ø44.450, ø33.338, ø31.111 — are smaller).
- **Length** — governed by the overall reference dimension **148.65 [5.852] REF**, measured from the rear face of the body to the front face of the nose.

**Note:** These are finished-envelope dimensions. For actual procurement you'd typically add machining allowance (e.g., Ø3.00 × 6.00 in bar of 8620) to permit cleanup on the OD, facing, and the ground/carburized surfaces (58–62 HRC, grind callout).
