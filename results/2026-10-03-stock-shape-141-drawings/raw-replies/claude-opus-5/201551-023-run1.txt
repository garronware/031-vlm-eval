## Bounding Stock — Round

This part is a flat, circular disc (a "mean master" gage plate) with a bolt‑hole pattern, so it would be cut from round bar/plate stock.

| Dimension | Metric | Inch |
|---|---|---|
| **Diameter (Dia)** | Ø83.570 mm (max OD, ±0.025) | Ø3.2902 in |
| **Length / Thickness (L)** | 2.485 mm (overall thickness) | 0.0978 in |

**Bounding cylinder: Ø83.57 mm × 2.485 mm (Ø3.2902 in × 0.0978 in)**

Notes on the call‑out:
- The Ø83.570 ±0.025 OD is the largest feature; the Ø76.2 [Ø3.00] and 1.505 [.0593] REF dimensions are interior/step features and do not affect the envelope.
- Material 8620, carburize/harden 58‑62 HRC, ground — so add appropriate finish stock (typically ~0.030–0.060 in on dia. and ~0.020–0.030 in on thickness) if ordering raw material: e.g. ~Ø3.375 in bar × ~0.13 in thick slice.
