This is a flat **base plate** (1020 steel) with chamfered corners, slots, tapped holes and dowel holes — all features are machined from flat plate, so rectangular bar/plate stock applies.

**Bounding box: 0.75 × 7.00 × 10.00 in (19.1 × 177.8 × 254.0 mm)**

Notes for stock selection:
- The 25.4 [1.00] × 45° corner chamfers (4 places) and the 3.2 [.13] × 45° edge break all-around are removed from the full rectangular envelope, so they don't increase the bounding size.
- Add finish allowance if ordering as-rolled plate (e.g., ~0.81–0.88 in thick stock) since the part calls for GRIND on thickness and 63 µin max finish; black oxide and stress relieve are post-machining operations and don't affect stock size.
