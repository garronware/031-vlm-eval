## Bounding Stock Determination

**Stock Type:** Round (cylindrical) — the part is a turned post with a constant outside diameter and tapped holes on each end along the axis.

### Bounding Dimensions

| Dimension | Inch | Metric |
|---|---|---|
| **Diameter** | Ø0.63 in | Ø15.9 mm |
| **Length** | 3.828 in | 97.23 mm |

**Callout:** Ø0.63 × 3.828 LG (Ø15.9 × 97.23 mm) — 1018 steel round bar

### Notes
- No features extend beyond the Ø0.63 outer surface; the 1/4‑20 × .50 deep tapped holes are internal at both ends, so they do not affect the envelope.
- Practical purchase stock would be Ø0.750 bar (next common size) if the OD is to be cleaned up in turning, with ~0.06–0.12 in extra length for facing/cutoff.
