## Bounding Stock — Round

This is a turned, axisymmetric part (stepped pin with a radiused nose), so it would be made from **round stock**.

| Dimension | Metric | Inch |
|---|---|---|
| **Diameter** | Ø8.310 mm | Ø0.3272 in |
| **Length** | 42.3 mm | 1.67 in |

**Basis:**
- **Dia:** Largest feature is the Ø8.310 [.3272] head/flange at the radiused (R3.175 [R.1250]) nose end — larger than the Ø6.35 [.250] body and Ø3.969 [.1563] tip.
- **Length:** Overall dimension 42.3 [1.67] spans the full part from nose to tip end.

*Practical purchase size:* Ø3/8 in (9.525 mm) bar × ~1.80 in cut length to allow for facing/cut‑off and grind stock (part is hardened A2 to 60‑63 HRC and ground).
