## Bounding Stock — Round

This part is a turned, rotationally‑based component (cylindrical head with a reduced‑diameter locating pin), so it would be produced from **round bar stock**.

| Parameter | Metric | Inch |
|---|---|---|
| **Diameter** | Ø25.4 mm | Ø1.00 in |
| **Length** | 50.8 mm | 2.00 in |

**Basis:**
- **Diameter** is set by the largest feature, the Ø25.4 [Ø1.00] head — all other features (Ø9.80 [.386] pin, Ø5.000 dowel holes, M5 tapped hole, diamond flats) fall within this envelope.
- **Length** is set by the overall dimension 50.8 [2.00] from the back face of the head to the end of the locator pin.

*Note: For manufacturing, add typical facing/cut-off allowance (≈ Ø27 mm × 54 mm / Ø1.06 × 2.12 in) since the part is ground after carburize/harden to 58–62 HRC.*
