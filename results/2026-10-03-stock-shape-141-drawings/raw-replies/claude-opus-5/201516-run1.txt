## Bounding Stock — Rectangular

This part is a solid rectangular block (bushing block), so it would be cut from **rectangular bar stock**.

| **Thickness** | 25.4 | 1.000 |

**Bounding box: 25.4 × 38.1 × 62.7 mm (1.000 × 1.500 × 2.468 in)**

How this is derived from the views:
- **Length 62.7 mm (2.468")** — overall dimension shown on the front/plan views.
- **Width 38.1 mm (1.500")** — end view: 22.5 mm above + 15.6 mm below the Ø7.5/Ø10 c'bore axis (also required to contain the 26.1 mm vertical hole spread of the 9.649/16.451 dimensions).
- **Thickness 25.4 mm (1.000")** — height shown on the middle elevation view.

Sanity check: 62.7 × 38.1 × 25.4 = 60.7 cm³ of 6061‑T6 ≈ 0.36 lb solid, which agrees with the 0.33 listed weight after the through/counterbored holes are removed.

*Note: material would typically be ordered as 1" × 1‑1/2" 6061‑T6 bar, cut ~2.6–2.7" long, or oversized slightly (e.g., 1.05 × 1.55 × 2.6) if all six faces are to be machined.*
