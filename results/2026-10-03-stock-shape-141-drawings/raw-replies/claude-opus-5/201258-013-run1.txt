## Bounding Stock — Round

This part is a circular flange/plate (Mean Master Plate), so it would be cut from round bar/disc stock.

| Dimension | Metric | Inch |
|---|---|---|
| **Diameter (Dia)** | Ø108.0 mm | Ø4.25 in |
| **Length / Thickness (L)** | 14.9 mm | 0.58 in |

**Basis:**
- Largest OD on the drawing is Ø108.0 [Ø4.25] (outer rim of plate).
- Overall height is the full section thickness 14.9 [.58]; the 10.570 [.4161] is a reference gaging dimension within that stack, and Ø95.021 and Ø60.0 THRU are internal features.

*Practical stock callout (with machining allowance): ≈ Ø112 mm (4.40 in) × 17 mm (0.67 in) long, material 8620, carb-hardened 58–62 HRC.*
