### Notes
- No features project beyond the rectangular envelope — all holes (Ø6.35 dowel/const. holes, #8‑32 tapped holes, 1/4 SHCS counterbores) are internal to the plate faces.
- Material is A‑2 tool steel, hardened 60‑63 HRC and ground, so the purchased blank would realistically be oversized (e.g., ~1.00 × 3.50 × 8.25 in) to allow for grind stock and black oxide finishing, but the **finished part envelope** is as listed above.
