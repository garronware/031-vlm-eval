## Bounding Stock — Round

This part is a turned disc (Ø83.57 [Ø3.290] flange) with an integral Ø12.7 [Ø.50] boss, so it would be made from **round stock**.

| Dimension | Metric | Inch | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Diameter** | Ø83.57 mm | Ø3.290 in | Outside diameter of flange/plate |
| **Length (axial)** | 25.4 mm | 1.00 in | Overall height, bottom gaging face to top of boss |

**Bounding cylinder: Ø83.57 × 25.4 mm (Ø3.290 × 1.00 in)**

Notes for ordering:
- Nearest common bar size would be **Ø3.50 in × 1.125 in** (8620 round bar) to allow cleanup on the OD and grind stock on the ground gaging faces (Dat. "W" / Dat. "Y") after carburize/harden to 58‑62 HRC.
- Plate web thickness is 9.5 [.38]; the 25.4 [1.00] controls the required slug length.
