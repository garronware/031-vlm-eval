## Stock Type: **Rectangular (cuboid) block**

The part is a vee block with a 45° chamfered corner, flat faces, and drilled/counterbored/tapped holes — all machined from a rectangular blank.

**Stock size: 2.750 × 3.19 × 4.00 in (69.85 × 81.0 × 101.6 mm)**

Notes for stock selection:
- Material is A/2 tool steel, hardened 58–60 HRC after rough machining, then ground — so add grind stock on finished faces.
- The 45° corner cut (with the 23.88 [.940] / 3.18 [.125] flat) is machined away from the full rectangular envelope, so it does not reduce the required blank size.
