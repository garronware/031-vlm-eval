## Bounding Stock — Round Stock

**Part:** MMC Pin #5 11 mm × 2 mm TP (Dwg. 200947, Rev 1)

| Parameter | Value (mm) | Value (in) |
|---|---|---|
| **Diameter** | Ø12.00 | Ø0.472 |
| **Length** | 145.0 | 5.709 |

**Bounding size: Ø12.00 mm × 145.0 mm**

**Basis:**
- The part is a turned, axially symmetric pin (round/cylindrical features only: Ø8.500, Ø9.80, Ø10.000, Ø12.00) — so round bar stock is the logical raw form.
- Largest outside diameter is the Ø12.00 collar/head near the right end, which sets the bounding diameter.
- Overall length is set by the 145.0 mm dimension from the 0 datum at the right end face to the left end face.

*Note: this is the finished bounding envelope. For actual bar stock ordering you'd typically add cleanup/facing allowance — e.g., Ø12.7 (1/2 in) × ~148 mm cut length.*
