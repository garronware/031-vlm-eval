The part is a turned, axisymmetric shaft (stepped diameters, ball nose, external M22×1.5 thread, internal M10×1.5 thread on centerline) — so it would be made from round bar.

| Bounding Dimension | Value | Driving Feature |

**Bounding cylinder ≈ Ø1.124 × 6.50 in**
