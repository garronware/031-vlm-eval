**Round stock (cylindrical bounding envelope)**

| Dimension | Value |
|---|---|
| Diameter | **Ø8.00 mm** (Ø8.000 / −0.005 ≈ 0.315 in) |
| Length | **90.00 mm** (≈ 3.543 in) |

**Rationale:** The part is a guide pin — an axisymmetric turned component. The largest diameter feature is the Ø8.000 −0.005 pin body (the Ø7.80 is a relieved/undercut section, and the two 1.00 mm deep flats/slots at 3.65–10.00 mm and 74.45–80.8 mm cut inward), so the controlling envelope diameter is 8.00 mm. The overall length dimension is 90.00 mm.

Bar stock callout would typically be Ø10 mm (next standard size) × ~95 mm to allow for cleanup and facing, but the finished-part bounding cylinder is **Ø8 × 90 mm**.
