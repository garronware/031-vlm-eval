## Bounding Stock — Round

This part is a turned/ground shaft (all features are concentric cylinders, end chamfers, a relief groove, a flat, tapped holes and dowel press‑fit holes on the ends), so it would be produced from **round bar stock (A‑2 tool steel)**.

| Bounding dimension | Metric | Inch |
|---|---|---|
| **Diameter** (largest OD — Ø25.400 ground surface) | **25.40 mm** | **1.0000 in** |
| **Length** (overall, end‑to‑end) | **88.90 mm** | **3.500 in** |

**Bounding cylinder: Ø25.40 × 88.90 mm (Ø1.000 × 3.500 in)**

*Note for purchasing/cut stock:* since the Ø1.0000 and Ø0.7500 surfaces are ground finish (S.F. in Det. #031/#064) and the part is hardened 60‑63 HRC, order oversize bar — typically **Ø1‑1/8 in × 3.75 in** (≈Ø28.6 × 95 mm) — to allow for grind stock and facing.
