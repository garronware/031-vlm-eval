## Bounding Envelope — Round Stock

This is a turned, rotationally‑symmetric part (flanged master post), so it would be made from round bar stock.

| Dimension | Metric | Inch |
|---|---|---|
| **Diameter** | Ø90.4 mm (flange OD) | Ø3.56 in |
| **Length** | ≈65.05 mm | ≈2.56 in |

**Bounding cylinder: Ø90.4 × 65.05 mm (Ø3.56 × 2.56 in)**

How the length is built up:
- Flange thickness: 7.9 mm [.31]
- Body/pilot length below flange face: 57.150 mm [2.2500] (REF, see ass'y)
- Total ≈ 65.05 mm [≈2.56]

Practical raw stock (8620, with cleanup allowance): roughly Ø3.625–3.75 in bar × ~2.75 in cut length.
