## Bounding Stock — Rectangular

This part is a prismatic vee block (flat faces, drilled/tapped holes, 45° vee relief cut on one corner), so it would be cut from **rectangular stock**.

**Bounding cuboid: 2.750 × 3.19 × 4.00 in (69.85 × 81.0 × 101.6 mm)**

Notes for stock selection:
- The 45° corner is a removed feature, so it does not reduce the bounding envelope.
- Material is A/2 (A2) tool steel, hardened 58–60 HRC and ground, so add grind stock (typ. +0.030–0.060 in per side) if ordering as-sawn/decarb-free blanks; oversize blank ≈ 2.81 × 3.25 × 4.06 in.
