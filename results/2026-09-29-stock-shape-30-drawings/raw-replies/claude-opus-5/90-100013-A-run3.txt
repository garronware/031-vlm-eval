## Bounding Envelope — Round Stock

**Part:** Handle, JRE Modular Hand Gauge (DWG 90‑100013 Rev A)

| Bounding Dimension | Value |
|---|---|
| **Diameter (Dia)** | **Ø1.500 in** |
| **Length (L)** | **4.96 in** |

### Basis

**Diameter** – Largest feature on the part is the knurled body OD, Ø1.5000 ±0.0015 (shown in the end view). All other diameters are smaller:
- Ø1.368 (left end flange)
- Ø1.1256 ±0.0005 (internal/step)
- Ø0.938, Ø0.850, Ø0.807, Ø0.374 (pilot/thread journals)

Since the knurl is a *raised* (displaced‑material) feature at 0.017 depth, the pre‑knurl turned diameter is under Ø1.500 — so Ø1.500 still governs the envelope.

**Length** – Taken from the "0" datum face:
- Left‑hand extreme: 3.83
- Right‑hand extreme: 1.13
- **Total = 3.83 + 1.13 = 4.96 in**

### Practical Stock Callout
6061‑T6 round bar, **Ø1.625 (1‑5/8") × 5.25 in** cut length, allowing cleanup on the OD and facing/parting stock on both ends.
