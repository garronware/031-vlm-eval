## Bounding Stock — Rectangular

This part is a rectangular prism (indicator block), so it would be cut from bar/plate stock.

| Dimension | Metric | Inch |
|---|---|---|
| **Thk** | 25.4 mm | 1.00 in |
| **W** | 28.6 mm | 1.12 in |
| **L** | 85.0 mm | 3.35 in |

**Bounding box: 25.4 × 28.6 × 85.0 mm (1.00" × 1.12" × 3.35")**

Basis:
- 25.4 [1.00] — overall depth, from the side elevation view
- 28.6 [1.12] — overall width, from the top/front elevation view
- 85.0 [3.35] — overall length/height, from the side elevation view

All other callouts (Ø12.73 gage bore at 71.00 from the base, #10‑32 threads, Ø6.350 dowel S.F. holes, 4.763/23.812 dowel locations) are internal features that fall inside this envelope.

Material is 1018 steel, black anodize on non‑gaging surfaces (per note), so add roughly 1.5–3 mm per side on the sawn blank if ordering stock for grinding.
