## Bounding Stock Assessment

**Stock form: ROUND (cylindrical)**

The part is a cylindrical "mean master" gage — its controlling outside feature is the Ø83.570 ±0.025 [3.2902] gaging diameter, with two flats (29.4 [1.16]) milled into the OD and a Ø30.042 [1.1828] cross‑bore. Since the full round OD is retained over most of the periphery, it must come out of round stock.

### Cylindrical bounding dimensions (finished part)

| **Dia** | 83.57 mm | 3.290 in |
| **L** | 56.9 mm | 2.240 in |

*(Across the flats the envelope necks down to ≈58.8 mm [2.32 in], but the bounding envelope is still set by the 83.570 diameter.)*

### Suggested rough stock
Because the OD is a ground/gaged surface (58–62 HRC, carburize‑harden, grind) allow clean‑up on all faces:

- **Ø 3.38 in (86 mm) × 2.38 in (60.5 mm) long** bar, 8620 steel
