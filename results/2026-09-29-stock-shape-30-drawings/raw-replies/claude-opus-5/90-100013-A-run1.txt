## Bounding Envelope — Round Stock

**Part:** Handle, JRE Modular Hand Gauge (DWG 90‑100013, Rev A)

| Dimension | Value |
|---|---|
| **Bounding Diameter** | **Ø 1.500 in** |
| **Bounding Length** | **4.96 in** |

### Basis

**Diameter (max feature):** Ø1.5000 ±0.0015 — the knurled handle body OD called out in the end view. All other diameters are smaller:
- Ø1.368 (rear flange/shoulder)
- Ø1.1256 ±0.0005 (internal/rear bore feature)
- Ø0.938, Ø0.850, Ø0.807, Ø0.374, and the 1‑14 UNF‑2A nose (≈Ø1.00 major)

*Note:* knurl depth is 0.017 cut **into** the surface (straight, 128 pitch), so it does not add to the envelope — though raised knurl material can grow the OD slightly, which is why a slightly oversize blank is standard.

**Length (from the 0 datum at the shoulder):**
- Rearward extent: 3.83 in
- Forward extent: 1.13 in (end of threaded nose)
- **Total = 3.83 + 1.13 = 4.96 in**

### Suggested Barstock
Ø1.75 in (1‑3/4) × 5.25 in of 6061‑T6 — allows cleanup on the Ø1.500 knurl diameter, facing stock on both ends, and workholding for the second-op/chuck-up.
