**Basis:**
- **Diameter** — governed by the largest feature on the part, the Ø10.0 mm head shown in the end view (all other diameters — Ø8.5 +0.050/0, Ø8.000 ±0.005, Ø7.80 — are smaller).

**Note:** This is the finished-part envelope. For actual purchasing you'd typically step up to the next standard bar size (e.g., 12 mm or 1/2 in AISI A2 drill rod/bar) and add ~3–6 mm to length for facing/workholding, since the part carries a DLC coating and ±0.005 mm diameter tolerance requiring finish grinding.
